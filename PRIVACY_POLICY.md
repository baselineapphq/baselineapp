# Privacy Policy — BaselineApp

**Last updated: March 2026**

## Overview

BaselineApp ("the App") is a baseball development tracking application developed for Android devices.

## Data Collection

**BaselineApp does not collect any personal data.**

All data entered into the App — including athlete profiles, session metrics, performance statistics, and profile photos — is stored exclusively on your device using the device's local storage. No data is transmitted to any server, third party, or external service.

## Data Storage

All App data is stored locally on your Android device in the App's private storage area. This data is:
- Not accessible by other apps on your device
- Not transmitted over the internet
- Not backed up to any cloud service by the App itself (your device's own backup settings may apply)
- Permanently deleted when you uninstall the App or use the in-app "Delete Account" feature

## Passwords

Account passwords created within the App are stored on-device using industry-standard PBKDF2 key derivation with 100,000 iterations and a random salt. Passwords are never stored in plain text and never transmitted.

## Children's Privacy

BaselineApp is designed for use by parents, coaches, and athletes. The App does not collect, transmit, or store any data externally, making it compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations.

## Permissions

The App may request the following device permission:

- **Read Media Images (Photos):** Required on devices running Android 12 or earlier to allow you to select a profile photo from your device's photo gallery. On Android 13 and later, the system photo picker is used and no permission is required. The App does not access, copy, or transmit your photos in any way — selected images are stored only within the App's private local storage on your device.

The App does **not** request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or any other device features.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a future version of the App, users will be notified via the App's update release notes.
